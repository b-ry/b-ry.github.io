A full site retheme for Stanford's research administration portal used by faculty, staff, and students across the university. I worked with a team on this one, taking the lead on several components — particularly those driving the content sections of the site.

- Owned development of several components, with a focus on the content-heavy sections of the site
- Built in Twig, SCSS, JavaScript, and PHP within a Drupal environment
- Handled Drupal core and module updates throughout the project
