The San Antonio Breast Cancer Symposium is one of the largest breast cancer research conferences in the world. I worked with a team to build out the WordPress site, taking ownership of several components throughout.

- Collaborated with the team across the site build, contributing to multiple components
- Implemented GSAP animations tied to scroll triggers — including sequential image fades, elements that slide and fade into view, and smooth gallery transitions
- Focused on making the interface feel alive and purposeful without being distracting
