Raleigh's site serves a large and diverse population, so getting the details right really matters. The work here touched several parts of the public-facing site as well as a couple of internal city platforms.

- __Services and Events__: Refined the Drupal views and Twig template overrides powering the Service Categories and Events displays, making it easier for residents to find what they need without unnecessary friction
- __Alert component__: Audited and updated the site-wide alert so it meets WCAG contrast requirements and announces correctly to screen readers
- __Accessibility across the site__: Worked through a range of issues across multiple components, from keyboard navigation and focus management to missing ARIA labels, bringing the site closer to WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- __Internal city platforms__: Updated modules and components on two separate internal city sites, addressing accessibility gaps and visual design updates to keep them consistent with the broader city brand
